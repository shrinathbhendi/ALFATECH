A SS Milk Boiler, also known as a milk boiling machine or milk cooker, is a commercial & industrial appliance used to heat and boil milk. It is designed to make the process of heating milk more convenient, efficient, and safe. Milk boilers are commonly used in households, cafes, restaurants, dairy processing plants, and food establishments where milk is a staple ingredient.
