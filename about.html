About Us

Dairy Equipment Manufacturer / Dealer / Consultancy

ESTABLISHED SINCE 1990

Alfa Tech India — A Legacy of Quality & Trust

Founded in 1990 by Mr. Milind Bhandarkar, Alfa Tech India is a premier brand of quality and trust across the Indian dairy industry. We combine advanced technology and engineering strength to deliver cutting-edge equipment for dairy farmers, collection centers, and processing plants.

Driven by over three decades of engineering excellence, our state-of-the-art facility in Pune manufactures high-performance Milking Machines, Automatic Milk Collection Systems (AMCS) with GPRS/GPS cloud integration, Ultrasonic Milk Analysers, Cream Separators, Khoya/Mawa Machines, Paneer Press Equipment, and complete Mini Dairy Plants.

Conferred with MINIRATNA status and recognized as an official NDDB Quality Approved Vendor (implementing QPM under the Ministry of Industry, Govt. of India), Alfa Tech India delivers heavy-duty equipment tailored for Indian conditions, serving over 100,000+ dairy farmers and 44+ major dairy cooperatives nationwide.
