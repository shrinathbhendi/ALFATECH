Alfa Tech India Whey Cream Separator

Price: Inquire for Direct Factory Price
  • Brand: Alfa Tech India
  • Processing Capacity: 500 Ltr/Hr (500 LPH)
  • Tank Capacity: 25 Litres
  • Material: Stainless Steel
  • Steel Grade: Food Grade SS 304
  • Operation: Continuous discharge of whey cream with concurrent liquid cleaning & solids bowl collection

Whey cream separators are used for removing cream from whey, which accrues during the production of cheese and paneer. It ensures continuous discharge of high-purity whey cream with concurrent clarification of separate liquid phases. Solids contained in raw whey or whey dust are efficiently collected in the bowl solids tank.
